Emigo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Emigo is a photo-sharing app that puts photos from your friends on your home screen widget. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

Emigo does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not use any analytics or advertising tracking software.

Who we are

Emigo is operated by the developer of Emigo. For any privacy question or request, contact abhisheksir6280@gmail.com.

What we collect

Information you give us

DataWhy we need it
Email addressTo create and sign in to your account, and to recover it.
Username and display nameSo friends can find you and see who sent a photo.
PasswordTo sign you in. Stored only as a cryptographic hash — we never store or see your actual password.
Profile photo (optional)Shown to your friends in the app.
Photos and captions you sendTo deliver them to the friends you choose. Captions are part of the image itself.

Information created as you use Emigo

DataWhy we need it
Your friends and friend requestsTo build your friends list and deliver photos to the right people.
Streaks and photo-exchange historyTo calculate streaks between you and each friend.
Saved recipient lists and pinned friendsShortcuts you create yourself for choosing who to send to.
Which photos you have viewed, and when you last opened ActivityTo show unread indicators correctly.
Blocks and reports you submitTo enforce blocking and to review reported content.

Technical information

DataWhy we need it
Push notification tokenA device identifier from Google Firebase, used only to send you notifications about photos and streaks.
IP addressUsed only momentarily to limit sign-in and sign-up attempts and prevent abuse. It is held in server memory, is never written to our database, and is discarded when the server restarts.
Google Play purchase token and subscription statusOnly if you buy Emigo Gold, to confirm your purchase with Google and unlock paid features.

What we do not collect

  • Your location.
  • Your contacts or address book.
  • Analytics, behavioural tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • Payment card details — payments are handled entirely by Google Play, and we never see your card.

Camera and photo access

Emigo asks for camera permission so you can take photos to send. If you subscribe to Emigo Gold, you may also choose photos from your gallery; in that case Emigo only receives the specific photos you pick. Emigo does not scan, read, or upload your photo library.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that make the app work. Each receives only what it needs, and none of them are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

ProviderWhat they handle
Cloudflare R2Stores the photo files themselves.
Google Firebase Cloud MessagingDelivers push notifications to your device.
Google Play BillingProcesses Emigo Gold purchases and confirms them to us.
NeonHosts our database.
RailwayHosts our server application.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where it is necessary to investigate abuse or protect someone's safety.

We never sell your personal data, and we never share it for advertising.

How long we keep things

  • Photos in the feed are removed automatically about 24 hours after they are sent, unless the recipient saves them to Memories.
  • Photos saved to Memories are kept until the person who saved them deletes them, or deletes their account.
  • Your account information is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • IP addresses used for rate limiting are never stored on disk.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your Emigo account from inside the app at any time:

Settings → Other → Delete account

Deleting your account removes your profile, your photos, your friendships, your streaks and your push notification tokens from our systems. This cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, email abhisheksir6280@gmail.com from your registered address and we will delete your account for you.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an Emigo Gold subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play — cancel from the Play Store's subscription settings.

Your rights

You can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct your name, username, email or profile photo from within the app.
  • Delete your account and its data, as described above.
  • Withdraw camera or notification permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Object to how we use your data, or ask us to restrict it.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the GDPR, or the CCPA. To exercise any of these, email us at the address above. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes. Access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will notify affected users of any breach that puts their data at risk.

Children

Emigo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process and store data on servers outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on those providers' standard contractual protections to keep your data safeguarded.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
