Emigo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Emigo is a photo-sharing application that displays photos from your friends on your home screen widget. This policy describes what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices and rights available to you.

Emigo does not sell your personal data, does not display advertising, and does not use analytics or advertising-tracking software of any kind.

Who we are

For any privacy-related question or request, please contact us at emigohq@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Technical information

DataPurpose

What we do not collect

  • Your location.
  • Your contacts or address book.
  • Analytics, behavioural tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • Payment card details — purchases are processed entirely by Google Play, and we never receive or store your card information.

Camera and photo access

Emigo requests camera permission so that you can take photos to send. If you subscribe to Emigo Gold, you may also choose photos from your device gallery; in that case, Emigo receives only the specific photos you select. Emigo does not scan, read, or upload the contents of your photo library.

How we share information

We share personal data only with service providers that are necessary to operate the app, and only to the extent required for them to perform their function. These providers are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

CategoryPurpose
Cloud storage providerStores the photo files you send within the app.
Push notification service (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging)Delivers notifications to your device.
Payment processor (Google Play Billing)Processes Emigo Gold purchases and confirms them to us.
Cloud hosting and database providersOperate the servers and databases that store your account information securely.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or where necessary to investigate suspected abuse or protect the safety of our users.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

Data retention

  • Photos in your feed are automatically removed approximately 24 hours after being sent, unless the recipient saves them to Memories.
  • Photos saved to Memories are retained until the person who saved them deletes them, or deletes their account.
  • Account information is retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • IP addresses used for abuse prevention are not stored long-term.

Deleting specific data without deleting your account

You do not need to delete your whole account to remove specific content. From within the Emigo app you can, at any time:

  • Delete a sent photo — open the photo and choose delete. This removes it permanently from our servers and from anyone it was sent to; it is not recoverable.
  • Remove a friend — this ends that friendship and stops any further photos being exchanged with them, without affecting your account or other friendships.
  • Block a user — prevents them from contacting you or seeing your activity, without deleting your account.

If you'd like us to delete a specific piece of data on your behalf and cannot do so from within the app, email emigohq@gmail.com from your registered address describing what to remove — we will action it within a reasonable time and confirm once done.

Deleting your account

You may permanently delete your Emigo account at any time from within the app:

Settings → Other → Delete account

Deleting your account removes your profile, photos, friendships, streaks, and notification tokens from our systems. This action cannot be undone. If you are unable to access the app, email emigohq@gmail.com from your registered address and we will delete your account on your behalf.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Emigo Gold subscription. Subscriptions are managed through Google Play and must be cancelled from the Play Store's subscription settings.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct your name, username, email address, or profile photo from within the app.
  • Delete your account and associated data, as described above.
  • Withdraw camera or notification permissions at any time through your device settings.
  • Object to certain uses of your data, or request that we restrict processing.

These rights may be available to you under applicable laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address above. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Security

Data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes, and access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel. While no system can be guaranteed completely secure, we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, and we will notify affected users in the event of a data breach that poses a risk to their personal data.

Children's privacy

Emigo is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so that we may remove it.

International data transfers

Our service providers may process and store information on servers located outside your country of residence. Where this occurs, we rely on those providers' contractual and technical safeguards to ensure your data remains protected in accordance with this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.
